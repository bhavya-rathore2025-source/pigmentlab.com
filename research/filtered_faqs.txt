EVALUATION & REPLACEMENT GUIDELINES
================================================================================
1. RELEVANT (KEEP / ADD):
   - Addresses subtractive paint/pigment physics, color ratios, color wheel harmonies,
     Itten RYB vs RGB, WCAG contrast, or color recipes (e.g. brown, skin tones, neutrals).
   - High search intent matching primary keywords for that language/tool.
2. IRRELEVANT (DROP / DO NOT ADD):
   - Computer science memory/byte calculations (e.g., bits per pixel in 256 colors).
   - Off-topic commercial/industrial applications we don't support (e.g. fabric dye brands).
   - Redundant or generic questions lacking search intent.
3. REPLACEMENT PROTOCOL:
   - When a relevant FAQ is approved to replace an existing page FAQ, we drop ONLY the
     irrelevant or weakest existing FAQ on that page, keeping strong ones intact.
   - Both the incoming FAQ and the replaced FAQ will be documented in original language
     AND English translation.
